This site hosts **From Scratch Automotive Engineering** — a collection of engineering books on building race cars from scratch.

Books are published **chapter by chapter** as they are written. Check the sidebar for what's available now; more is added over time.

## The series

Each book in the series focuses on a different part of vehicle design and build. The first book, [*Fundamentals in Formula*]({{ "/books/fundamentals-in-formula/" | relative_url }}), covers the fundamentals of putting a Formula Student car together.

## About the author

I am an automotive engineer working in Electrical Systems at Rivian. I founded the current BYU Formula E team in 2022 and now I write about getting started in Formula Student and Automotive Engineering. I post my weekly articles to [*Substack*](https://fsautoeng.substack.com/) in the newsletter "The Contact Patch". The weekly articles are basically my progress in writing the mini-series for Formula Student books. I also help manage a community on [*Hideout*](https://enterhideout.app/c/fsae), which is like Duolingo but for learning about Formula Student/FSAE. I'm always happy to connect and help out students/teams when I can, drop me a note on [*LinkedIn*](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brad-j-nelson/) anytime.
