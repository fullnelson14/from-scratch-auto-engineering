This book will cover the tricky task of developing math models to predict how your car will perform. As with all subjects that I attempt to write about, the field is deep here and extends well beyond my knowledge. Still, the principles here will give you a good start and will result in you writing your very own custom programs to simulate an FSAE car around a track.
