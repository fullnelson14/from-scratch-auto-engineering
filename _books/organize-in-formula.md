This book is about starting the club organization. Starting an FSAE team is a chicken before the egg problem - you usually need a car to start recruiting a lot of people, but you need a lot of people before you can build a car… tough. We'll lay out the strategies that I have seen work to recruit well and retain well, as well as how to structure your team into effective groups.
