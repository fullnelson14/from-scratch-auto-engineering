This book is meant to be the tactics and tools for designing the systems taught about in Learn in Formula. In my experience, learning about the working principles of a system and trying to design one for the first time are very different experiences, so this book is my efforts at trying to lay out the details on how you actually go about turning your knowledge and ideas into a manufacturable design. We'll cover product development basics and the stages that your design will need to pass through in order to become mature enough to build.

This book is currently being written. Chapters will appear in the sidebar as they are released.
